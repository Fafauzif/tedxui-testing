# Letters to Grow design

Readers land on a cream letter sheet, with an olive identity rail on wide screens and a compact masthead on phones. The rail uses the supplied TEDxUniversitasIndonesia mark, a pressed flower and the Still, We Grow campaign identity. Paper and text remain clear while the ornamental artwork loads.

Source Serif 4 carries long reading. Cormorant Garamond sets the display typography. DM Sans keeps the controls legible. The local font files and their licences ship with the build.

Share to Story presents the real 1,080 × 1,920 PNG. A scalloped heart and olive print texture frame the exact excerpt, byline and supplied mark. The same bytes go to the preview, download and supported native file share. The letter link is copied separately for Instagram’s link sticker.

The writer and Community letters use the same cream sheet and display typography. The writer previews the public text and picks a Story line before publishing. The collection shows recent public submissions with permanent links.

# Artwork and fonts

Original project assets generated for this revision using built-in image generation, then resized and compressed to WebP for website delivery. Revision 3 uses a 1024px board texture, 928×1200px paper and 560px chalk envelope. Original generated PNG sources are retained outside the deployment ZIP. Generated imagery contains no contributor portraits or real handwriting. All letter text and UI labels remain HTML.

## Asset prompts

- **black-paper.webp:** Square flatbed scan of dark black photocopied construction paper, subtle fine white/charcoal xerox grain and uneven ink density. Edge-to-edge, orthographic, near-black. No objects, words, symbols, borders, vignette or lighting effects.
- **letter-paper.webp:** Blank portrait off-white uncoated writing sheet, fully visible on transparent background, small deckled edges, fine fibre and one faint fold. Center kept clean for HTML text. No writing, stamps, objects, perspective tilt or scene.
- **envelope-chalk.webp:** Original open envelope and blank letter drawn in creamy-white chalk/wax crayon, thick imperfect outlines, pencil hatching, two vermilion marks, playful photocopied-zine treatment, transparent background. No words, logos, faces, eyes or copied brand artwork.

Visual reference: Unlikely Collaborators for high contrast, expressive type and drawn material; Reasons to Stay for immediate intimate letter reading. No source website artwork or writing copied.

Fonts bundled under upstream Open Font Licenses: Barlow Condensed, Courier Prime, Caveat and DM Sans. Revision 1 font files/licenses remain in the replacement package for compatibility but unused files make no runtime request.
